# VCB E-Memo — Document Control Hub

A unified document register for VCB Group's active construction projects, plus a
portal where staff submit letters and browse what has been logged.

Two sources fill the register: an automated logger that files documents arriving
by email, and manual entries made in the portal. The portal is also the read
interface the team browses day to day.

## What it does

- **Document register** — one searchable record across all active projects
- **Automatic filing** — documents arriving by email are logged without anyone
  re-typing them
- **Manual submission** — staff submit letters directly, with the document
  number issued for them
- **Approval and signature** — approvers act from the record, and a signed
  PDF is produced per memo
- **Sign-in gated** — the register is not public

## Notes
