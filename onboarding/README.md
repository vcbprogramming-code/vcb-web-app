# VCB 90-Day Onboarding Portal

Walks new hires at Vichitbhan Construction through their first 90 days — the
paperwork to hand in, the department they are joining, and what to learn in each
of the first three months.

## What it does

- **Required documents** — the list a new hire must submit, with a place to
  upload each one and a tick when it is done
- **Department selection** — the checklist adapts to the department and to
  whether the hire is junior or senior
- **Three 30-day phases** — 0–30, 30–60 and 60–90 days, each a checklist of what
  to learn and who to meet
- **Progress that persists** — a hire can close the page and come back to where
  they left off
- **Org chart** — who is who in the department they are joining
- **Admin editor** — HR can reword or add checklist items without touching code
- **Completion celebration** when the 90 days are finished

## Notes

An orientation aid, not an HR monitoring tool. Progress is there to help the new
hire keep their place, not to report on them.
