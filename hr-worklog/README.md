# HR Daily Work Log

บันทึกการทำงานรายวัน — a daily record of what each employee worked on, across
VCB Group's construction sites (โครงการ) and head office.

Site staff fill in the day's work; HR and management read across sites to see
coverage, workload and cost by activity.

## What it does

- **Daily entry by site and month** — a grid of employees against the days of
  the month, filled in from a searchable list of work activities
- **A main task and an optional second task** per person per day. A day with
  both filled is still **one manday** — the second slot is extra work, not a
  second shift
- **Work index** — the master list of activities, each with a code, category and
  cost mapping
- **Leave requests** — submitted, then approved or declined, and reflected in
  the grid
- **Dashboards** — completeness and coverage per site, comparing units
- **Employee moves** between sites, kept as a record rather than a silent edit
- **Excel export** for a site's month, the work index, or the cost index
- **Edit window** — entries cannot be filled more than a day ahead, and older
  days lock after a few days for anyone but an admin

## Notes

The whole app is open — there is no login. Anyone with the link can read and
write any site. This is deliberate: site staff share devices and an account step
was judged more likely to stop the log being filled in than to protect it.

Every change is recorded with what it was before, so an edit can always be
traced.
